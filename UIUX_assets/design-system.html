04Usage Notes

Color Discipline

Ivory + ink for 90% of surface. Red is reserved for primary action and parody stamps. Gold is a single-use accent — never two golds in one band.

Type Rhythm

Mono = metadata. Sans = UI. Display italic = parody display moments only. Body copy never wider than 70ch.

Parody Hygiene

Every roast surface carries a visible PARODY stamp. Avatar is original line art. No real platform marks.
